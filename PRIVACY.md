# Privacy

Space Swipe Lab processes gesture events locally on your Mac.

- It does not collect analytics or telemetry.
- It does not transmit gesture, application, window, or usage data.
- It does not contain advertising or tracking SDKs.
- It stores only local preferences such as transition speed, swipe-override state, and menu-bar visibility.
- Accessibility permission is used only to observe, suppress, and synthesize the horizontal gesture events required to switch Spaces.

The application currently makes no network requests.
